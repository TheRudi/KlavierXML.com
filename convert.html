Option 05

PDF2XML on KlavierXML.com

Upload a score PDF. KlavierXML converts it to MusicXML so you can open it in the Klavier app or share it on the MusicXML exchange.

Convert

Score PDF → MusicXML

Use a scanned score PDF, or a PDF you built from JPG pages (see How to get songs, option 04). Please only upload music you have the right to convert.

Conversion runs on klavierxml.com. You will get a downloadable .xml when it finishes.

Next

After you download

  1. Save the MusicXML on your iPad.
  2. Open the file from the Klavier app.
  3. Or upload it to the MusicXML exchange so others can download it.
